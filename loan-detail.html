Personal Loan

$2,000 to $50,000 over one to seven years, at a rate that is fixed the day you sign and never moves again. No origination fee, no early settlement charge.

What people use it for

Most of our personal loans go towards three things: clearing higher-cost card debt, covering a one-off cost like a move or a wedding, or spreading a bill that arrived without warning. Because the loan is unsecured, your home and car are never used as collateral — the agreement rests on your income and credit history alone.

Eligibility criteria

Everything below is checked with a soft search first, which leaves no mark on your file.

  • Aged 18 or over and resident here
  • Three years of address history
  • Regular income, employed or self-employed
  • No bankruptcy in the last six years
  • A current account in your own name
  • Repayments affordable after existing commitments

What you will need to upload

  • Government photo ID — passport or driving licence
  • Three months of bank statements for your main account
  • Latest payslip, or two years of accounts if self-employed
  • Proof of address dated within the last three months
