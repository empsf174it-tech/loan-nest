Three former bank underwriters, one shared frustration, and a decade spent proving that transparent lending can also be a good business.

Our story

We kept declining people who deserved a yes

Between us we had spent twenty-two years inside credit teams at large banks. The pattern was always the same: a scorecard nobody could explain would decline a nurse with a fifteen-year work history because of one missed phone bill in 2011, while the advertised rate on the billboard outside applied to almost nobody who walked through the door.

LoanNest opened in Portland in 2014 with eleven staff and a single product. The rule we wrote on the wall that first week has not changed: show the total cost before the application, and let a person read anything the model cannot.

Our mission

Make the true cost of borrowing obvious before anyone commits to it.

Who we serve

186,000 households and small businesses across 34 states, and counting.

How we earned the right to lend

Regulatory approval is slow, expensive and the reason our customers can sleep at night.

  1. 2014

    Consumer lender licence granted

    LoanNest opens in Oregon with eleven staff, one personal loan product and NMLS registration #000000.

  2. 2016

    Licensed across twelve states

    Multi-state approval completed, with a dedicated compliance function reporting directly to the board.

  3. 2018

    First SOC 2 Type II audit passed

    Independent assurance over security, availability and confidentiality controls, repeated annually since.

  4. 2021

    One billion dollars lent

    Reached with a default rate below the industry average and no change to the no-fee promise.

  5. 2023

    Secured lending arm authorised

    Mortgage and home equity products approved, each with a named underwriter assigned from offer to completion.

  6. 2025

    Fair collections charter published

    Our forbearance standards written down publicly and audited by an independent consumer advocacy panel.
